How we think about buildings.

Start with the site

Slope, light, wind and the street come first. A building that fits its ground needs less to look right.

Use honest materials

Brick, timber, steel and concrete, left to age. We choose what will look better in thirty years, not just at handover.

Design to be repaired

Every detail can be reached, replaced and fixed. That is what keeps a building in use after the first owner has gone.
