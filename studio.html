We are twenty-four architects, designers and makers who believe a good building is one that is still loved when nobody is looking at it.

How a project runs.

  1. Listen

    We walk the site and spend time with the people who will use the building before we draw anything.

  2. Draft

    Models and sketches, reviewed together every week, until the plan is clear enough to build.

  3. Build

    We stay on site through construction, so what is drawn is what gets built.

  4. Care

    A year after handover we come back, see how the building is used, and fix what needs fixing.
